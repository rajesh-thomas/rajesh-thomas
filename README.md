# Dr. Rajesh Thomas

Cybersecurity researcher and academic working on AI-assisted threat intelligence, adversary simulation, and GRC-aware security.

## Research focus

- **Offensive security:** red teaming, adversary simulation, and threat intelligence
- **AI/ML for cybersecurity:** intrusion and botnet detection, DNS exfiltration analysis, network traffic visualization, and forensic analysis of cyber threats, with the aim of detecting adversarial behavior at early stages
- **Governance, risk and compliance (GRC):** integrating regulatory, risk-management and compliance requirements into technical security frameworks, for national and enterprise contexts

## Where my research is heading

Advancing AI-assisted threat intelligence, adversary simulation, and GRC-aware security architectures that connect offensive security insights with compliant, resilient, industry-aligned defense.
